About Reliable Engineering LLC

At Reliable Engineering Services, we are a passionate team with over 50 years of combined industry expertise. Our mission is to deliver innovative, dependable solutions to our esteemed clients.

As an ISO 9001:2015-certified, 100% minority-owned company, we specialize in transforming ideas into reality—from concept to production. With facilities spanning 70,000 sq. ft. in Chenoa, USA, and 50,000 sq. ft. in Nashik, India, we ensure flexibility as both on-shore and off-shore partners.

Our state-of-the-art, paperless ERP-enabled factories are EDI-capable, enabling seamless operations. Trust us for quality, precision, and reliability in manufacturing.

Founded in 1976 in Chenoa, Illinois, our company began its journey with a clear mission: to deliver quality engineering solutions. Our first major milestone came when we partnered with CATERPILLAR, laying the groundwork for our legacy in the manufacturing industry.

In 2014, Kishor Salunkhe took ownership of the company, bringing a bold vision for growth and innovation. Under his leadership, we expanded our customer base to include global giants like KOMATSU and NAVISTAR. To further strengthen our global presence, we established a second, cutting-edge facility in Nashik, India, in 2021.

Today, with a strong foothold in the United States and India, we proudly supply high-quality components to leading automotive and earth-moving vehicle OEMs worldwide. Our journey reflects our unwavering commitment to innovation, precision, and excellence in engineering solutions.
